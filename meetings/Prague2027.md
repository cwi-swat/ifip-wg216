# Prague 2027

Welcome to the website for the 19th meeting of the IFIP TC2 Working Group 2.16 on Language Design.

**Dates**: Monday, February 1 to Friday, February 5, 2027.

**Place**: Prague, Czech Republic

**Organizer**: Tomas Petricek, Charles University

More information about the meeting and instructions for registering will appear here soon.
In the meantime, if you have any questions, send me a message at
[tomas@tomasp.net](mailto:tomas@tomasp.net?subject=[WGLD]%20question).

## Meeting Details

* **Dates:** February 1–5, 2027
* **Location:** Room S1 (4th floor), Malostranské náměstí 25, Prague
* **Registration:** Registration instructions to appear

## Location in Prague

The meeting will take place in the Informatics building of the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics,
Charles University at **Malostranské náměstí 25, Praha 1**. Note that the faculty has multiple
buildings in different locations in Prague!
