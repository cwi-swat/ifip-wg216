# Prague 2027

Welcome to the website for the 19th meeting of the IFIP TC2 Working Group 2.16 on Language Design, hosted at Charles University in Prague.


**Dates**: Monday, February 1 to Friday, February 5, 2027  
**Place**: Prague, Czech Republic  
**Address:** Room S1 (4th floor), Malostranské náměstí 25, Prague  
**Organizer**: Tomas Petricek, Charles University

**Registration instructions to appear in November.** We will ask you to register and pay registration fee to cover the costs in advance.
In the meantime, if you have any questions or suggestions for useful information
to include, send me a message at
[tomas@tomasp.net](mailto:tomas@tomasp.net?subject=[WGLD]%20question).

## Location in Prague

Charles University at **Malostranské náměstí 25, Praha 1**. Note that the faculty has multiple
buildings in different locations in Prague! You need the one on the map below.

## Accommodation

There are many hotels within walking distance of the meeting venue in Malá Strana (Lesser Town).
Please book directly with the hotel; prices are approximate
per-night rates and may vary depending on the room type and date of booking.

**Recommended hotel.** Many visitors have stayed at the following hotel, so this is our
recommended first choice if you want to stay in the same hotel as others:

- [Hotel Pytloun Kampa Garden](https://www.pytlounkampagardenhotel.cz/en/), ~12 min walk, 90–140 €  
  U Sovových mlýnů 9, 118 00 Praha 1

**Other alternatives.** The following may be interesting if you want something more or less
expensive or even closer to the department:

- [Hotel U Schnellů](https://www.uschnellu.cz/hotel), < 1 min walk, 100–140 €  
  Tomášská 2, 118 00 Praha 1
- [Hotel Pod Věží](https://www.podvezi.com/cs/), ~4 min walk, 140–210 €  
  Mostecká 2, 118 00 Praha 1
- [Pension Dientzenhofer](http://www.dientzenhofer.cz/), ~10 min walk, 80–130 €  
  Nosticova 2, 118 00 Praha 1

**Alternative accommodation.** There are also plenty of other accommodation options
in Prague including Airbnb and appartments on Booking.com. Public transport is reliable
and widely used by all locals, so you can easily find many options within a few minute
tram ride radius.
